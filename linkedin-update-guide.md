### What NOT to Post:
- Generic motivational content
- Memes or off-topic content
- Anything that makes you sound like you're job hunting desperately

### Posting Frequency:
- 1-2 times per week
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Share insights, not achievements without context

## 14. Red Flags to Watch For

**In recruiter conversations, watch for:**

❌ Companies that want "someone who can code everything from scratch"
❌ Roles that emphasize "10+ years of hands-on coding"
❌ Job descriptions with 50+ required technologies
❌ Companies that view AI tools as "cheating"
❌ Startups with no product-market fit looking for a miracle worker

✅ Companies that value speed and business impact
✅ Roles emphasizing "problem solving" and "solution design"
✅ Teams already using AI tools (GitHub Copilot, etc.)
✅ Companies with clear problems to solve
✅ Product-focused organizations that measure ROI
