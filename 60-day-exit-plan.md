## Week 5-6: Target 10 Companies with Direct Outreach

### Task 5: Research and Identify 10 Target Companies
**Time: 5 hours (30 min per company)**

**Criteria for selection:**
- ✅ Remote-first or remote-friendly
- ✅ Building products (SaaS, developer tools, B2B platforms)
- ✅ Actively hiring for roles matching your skills
- ✅ Salary range matches or exceeds current comp
- ✅ Company size: 20-500 employees (small enough to be agile, big enough to be stable)
- ✅ Tech stack includes: Python, Go, TypeScript, React, AI/LLM integration

**Where to find them:**
- AngelList (filter: remote, hiring, tech stack)
- LinkedIn Jobs (filter: remote, save companies)
- Y Combinator company directory
- Ask former colleagues where they went
- Companies you've heard of doing interesting work

**For each company, research:**
- What products do they build?
- What problems do they solve?
- Who are the engineering/product leaders? (Find on LinkedIn)
- What's their tech stack? (Check job descriptions, engineering blog)
- What have they posted about recently? (LinkedIn, blog, Twitter)
- What challenges might they be facing? (Scaling, new features, team growth)

### Task 8: Prepare for Interviews
**Time: 4 hours**

**Create your "greatest hits" stories:**

**Story 1: LegislationVUE (AI Integration)**
- Problem: Legal team spending $40K/year
- Your approach: User interviews, technical research, AI experimentation
- Technical decisions: Why Azure OpenAI, prompt engineering approach, data architecture
- Challenges: AI consistency, search performance, user adoption
- Result: $40K saved, 6 states covered, positive user feedback
- Lesson: What you learned about AI in production

**Story 2: Building 6 Products (Product Ownership)**
- Problem: Organization had multiple workflow gaps
- Your approach: Identify highest-impact problems, build MVPs, iterate
- Challenges: Limited resources, changing requirements, user adoption
- Result: $400K in total savings, 800+ daily users
- Lesson: What you learned about prioritization and shipping

**Story 3: Multi-Tenant Architecture (Technical Depth)**
- Problem: FacilityVUE needed to serve 100+ isolated school districts
- Your approach: Database schema design, data isolation, performance considerations
- Technical decisions: PostgreSQL schemas vs. separate databases, access control
- Challenges: Performance at scale, data migration, security
- Result: Successfully serving 100+ tenants
- Lesson: What you learned about SaaS architecture

## Week 9-10: More Outreach + Interview Process

### Task 10: Second Wave of Outreach (10 More Companies)
**Time: 5 hours**

If you haven't gotten 3+ interviews from first wave:
- Repeat Task 5 and Task 6 with 10 new companies
- Refine your message based on what worked/didn't work in first wave
- Try different types of companies or roles

### Task 11: Interview and Negotiate
**Time: Variable**

**When you get interview requests:**

**Phone screen prep:**
- Research the interviewer on LinkedIn
- Prepare 3 questions about the role/company
- Have your stories ready
- Be ready to talk about salary expectations (know your number)

**Technical interview prep:**
- Review your VUES architecture
- Be ready to whiteboard system design
- Prepare to discuss AI integration decisions
- Brush up on algorithms if needed (LeetCode easy/medium)

**Final round prep:**
- Prepare questions for the team
- Understand their product deeply
- Think about what you'd prioritize in first 90 days
- Have salary range ready

**Negotiation:**
- Never give a number first
- When pushed: "I'm looking for something in line with market rate for this role and my experience. What's the range for this position?"
- If they give a number: "That's in the range I was considering. Can you tell me more about the full compensation package?"
- Get multiple offers if possible (leverage)

## The Mindset

**This is not about finding A job.**
**This is about finding THE RIGHT job.**

You've been at MOREgroup for 12 years. The next place needs to be:
- Remote (non-negotiable)
- Values builders (not just efficiency optimizers)
- Has real problems to solve (not just maintenance)
- Pays fairly (match or exceed current)
- Room to grow (not a dead end)

**You're interviewing them as much as they're interviewing you.**

**Don't settle.** If Platform Manager is your bridge for 12 months while you find the right thing, that's fine.

But you're not staying at MOREgroup. You're leaving. The only question is when and where to.
