- AI-first or AI-native company
- Emphasizes speed and innovation
- Looking for "builders" or "0-to-1" people

**Example 2 (Enterprise):** Use when company is:
- Established enterprise (Fortune 500, large corp)
- Modernizing legacy systems
- Emphasizes stability and reliability
- Looking for experience with compliance, security, scale

**Example 3 (AI-First):** Use when company is:
- Building AI products or infrastructure
- Job description mentions AI/ML heavily
- Looking for "AI Solutions Architect" or similar title
- Values modern development approaches

**Example 4 (Remote-First):** Use when company is:
- Fully remote or remote-first
- Building tools for distributed teams
- Values async communication
- Emphasizes work-life balance or flexibility

## Red Flags in Job Descriptions

**Don't apply (or heavily customize) if the job description:**
- Requires "10+ years of hands-on coding in X language"
- Lists 50+ required technologies
- Emphasizes "coding from scratch" or "no AI tools"
- Focuses on specific algorithms or low-level optimization
- Requires on-site 5 days/week (unless you're willing)
- Sounds like they want a code monkey, not an architect

**DO apply if the job description:**
- Emphasizes "problem solving" and "business impact"
- Mentions "architecture", "design", "strategy"
- Values speed and iteration
- Talks about AI/ML or modern development practices
- Focuses on outcomes over outputs
- Mentions remote-first or flexible work

## Common Objections & How to Address Them

### Objection: "We need someone who can code everything from scratch."

**Response:**
"I can code, but I use AI tools to accelerate implementation so I can focus on architecture and business impact. In the past 18 months, I've shipped 6 production applications this way—same quality, 10x faster. Happy to discuss specific technical challenges where you think this approach might not work."

### Objection: "This sounds like ChatGPT wrote your code."

**Response:**
"You're right—I use Claude and GitHub Copilot to accelerate development. But I own the architecture, design decisions, and product strategy. The code is one piece of delivering business value. I can walk you through any technical decision in my portfolio and explain why I chose that approach."

### Objection: "We need 10+ years of experience with [SPECIFIC TECHNOLOGY]."

**Response:**
"I have [X] years with [TECHNOLOGY], but more importantly, I have 12 years of experience choosing the right tech stack for the problem, architecting systems that scale, and delivering measurable ROI. I've learned [LIST OF TECHNOLOGIES] in the past 18 months because the problems required them. I'm a fast learner who focuses on outcomes."

### Objection: "Can you explain how this function works?" (pointing to code)

**Response:**
"Let me walk you through the business logic and architecture decision here. [EXPLAIN WHAT IT DOES AND WHY]. For the specific implementation details, I worked with AI on this—I can revisit it and explain the code-level specifics, but my strength is the system design and business logic, not memorizing every line."
